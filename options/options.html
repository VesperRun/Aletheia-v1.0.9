Aletheia

Aletheia runs entirely in your browser. It does not send page content to remote servers, and it does not collect browsing telemetry. It is conservative by design: it may miss some clutter because restraint is part of trust. Treat its heuristics as “likely clutter” helpers, not perfect detectors.

My defaults

These apply on every site unless you choose Clarify, Allow, or per-site overrides in the floating panel.

Safe custom rules

Add conservative CSS selectors scoped to a hostname. Rules are evaluated locally when pages load and as the page updates. If a selector is too broad, you may hide something important—start small.

    Texas-facing privacy & AI disclosures

    Not legal advice. Texas law changes, and what applies depends on who publishes Aletheia, how it is distributed, and what data you actually process. If you ship this commercially, have a Texas-licensed attorney review your notices and practices.

    • Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) (Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 541): Aletheia’s reference design stores extension settings locally in the browser and does not add Aletheia-operated remote telemetry as part of its core architecture. If TDPSA applies to you as a controller, you should still publish a clear privacy notice (often at an HTTPS URL for the Chrome Web Store) and provide a contact path for consumer requests where required.
    • Texas AI-related statutes (examples include laws addressing deepfakes and certain harmful synthetic media): Aletheia is a client-side display helper. It is not designed to generate deceptive media, sexual deepfakes, or impersonation workflows.
    • Children: Aletheia is not intended as a “directed to children” product; parental controls and browser profiles remain the primary safeguard for minors.
    • Your controls: You can remove site overrides, delete custom rules, clear extension data in Chrome, or uninstall Aletheia to remove locally stored settings on that profile.

    Permissions (why they exist)

    • storage saves your settings on this device.
    • <all_urls> host access lets Aletheia register a local content script on pages you visit. It is not used to exfiltrate data.
    • activeTab lets Aletheia read the active tab’s hostname when you click the toolbar icon.
    • The background service worker only forwards a local keyboard shortcut to the active tab. It does not open network connections.
